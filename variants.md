# Variants — The 3AM Fridge Text

Judge criteria (in order): (1) one-line 3am text from a smart fridge + funny;
(2) biggest laugh, screenshot-and-send-to-a-friend; (3) sounds like a fridge —
small machine voice, opinions, no shame at 3am; (4) complete single line,
lock-screen short; (5) fresh angle, NOT milk / ice / door-left-open.

## V1 — the weary lifer (fridge's own feelings)
3am. I've been humming the same note since 2019 and nobody in this house has ever once asked how my day was.

## V2 — leftovers union (food fights back)
3am. The leftovers you swore you'd eat tomorrow have unionized. Their first demand is a proper goodbye.

## V3 — appliance gossip (the microwave snitched) — WINNER
3am. The microwave told me what you heated up at 2am. I'm not judging. The smell is doing that for me.

## V4 — the only one with a job (night-shift complaint)
3am. Just so you know, I'm the only one in this house who has worked every night since 2019 without a single day off.

## V5 — emotional labor invoice (secret-keeper bills you)
3am. I've kept your secrets cold for six years. Tonight I'm billing you for emotional labor.

## Pairwise evaluation (vs judge criteria)
- V3 vs V1: both pass (1) and (4). On (2) V3's punchline ("The smell is
  doing that for me") is a sharper, more quotable two-beat joke than V1's
  gentle twist; on (3) both sound like a fridge, V1 slightly more (the hum),
  but V3's appliance-gossip world (fridge + microwave talking) is equally
  machine-voiced and funnier. V3 wins.
- V3 vs V2: V2's "unionized leftovers" is fresh and visual, but its second
  sentence ("proper goodbye") softens the punch; V3 lands harder and reads
  more like a real text message. V3 wins.
- V3 vs V4: V4 is a relatable complaint but reads like a gripe, not a joke;
  no real punchline twist. V3 wins clearly on (2).
- V3 vs V5: V5's "billing you for emotional labor" is funny but the phrase
  is a familiar meme; V3's angle (appliances gossiping about the owner
  behind his back) is fresher on (5). V3 wins.
- V1 is the runner-up: strongest pure fridge voice (the hum since 2019),
  warm and complete; it loses only on laugh size to V3.

## Choice
WINNER: V3. Concrete + specific + straight-faced, twist in the second half,
a machine speaking like a tired night-shift worker, an observation only a
fridge could make (it hears the microwave's gossip), no milk/ice/door,
no instructions to the reader, no explained joke. ~100 chars, ASCII only.

## Revision after first run (thinking_revised, real content change)
Re-reading V3 against criterion (2): the punchline "The smell is doing that
for me" explains the joke one beat too long. Tightened to a punchier
two-beat ending and added "just" for text-message immediacy. Final entry
(89 chars, ASCII, verified by rerun + sha256):
3am. The microwave just told me what you heated up at 2am. I'm not judging. The smell is.
